A LITTLE CLARITY

Good to know.

Personal service, from the first question.

Are these original designer perfumes?

No. This is an independent collection of inspired fragrances. Brand and fragrance names identify scent inspirations only. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by those brands, and do not sell their original products.

How do I place an order?

Add fragrances to your selection, then send your enquiry on WhatsApp. We’ll discuss availability, price, bottle size, payment and delivery before you confirm an order. This website does not take payments.

Can I customise my fragrance?

Share your preferred inspiration and the style you enjoy on WhatsApp. Available customisation options and any additional costs will be confirmed individually.

What sizes, prices and delivery options are available?

Please enquire for current sizes, concentration, pricing, availability, delivery areas, timelines and returns terms. These details must be confirmed before ordering.
